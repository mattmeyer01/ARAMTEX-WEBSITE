INOX 304 stainless steel press fittings

We are adding the Besco INOX 304 line of press fittings in the M profile to our range: up to 16 bar, from −10 to 110 °C.

  • Fast installation without a flame: joints are pressed cold, with no welding, soldering or gluing.
  • Drinking water and heating: corrosion-resistant stainless steel, CE marking, PZH hygiene certificate.
  • Full range: diameters 15–108 mm, 25 product groups and 287 items.
  • Common M profile: the fittings are pressed with M jaws, which many installers already have for their press tools.
  • Larger diameters: our range also includes INOX 316L up to 168.3 mm, with DVGW and WRAS approvals.

Technical questions

Good to know.

Answers based on the manufacturer’s documentation. Our sales team will explain the rest.

Call: 513 191 502
Which pipes does Tectite connect?

According to the manufacturer’s compatibility table: Sprint and Classic connect copper pipes as well as PB and PEX with a liner. Pro additionally connects galvanised carbon steel. Tectite 316 connects copper, stainless steel and carbon steel, and Carbon connects carbon steel.

Which fittings can be dismantled?

Classic, Pro and 316 are demountable. Sprint and Carbon are non-demountable.

What are the operating parameters?

Tectite Classic with copper pipe: up to 16 bar, from −24 to 95 °C. Limits for other pipes and lines are given in the temperature and pressure table in the manufacturer’s catalogue.

What warranty does the manufacturer give?

25 years on Tectite Sprint, Classic, Pro and 316 with other manufacturers’ pipes and 30 years with Yorkshire pipes and recommended pipes, provided installation complies with the instructions.

Which valves are in the system?

PT550 ball valve (15–54 mm), PT913 Y-strainer (15–54 mm), TX300 valve (15 and 22 mm), TX405 mixing valve (15 and 22 mm) and TX480 isolating valve (15 mm).
