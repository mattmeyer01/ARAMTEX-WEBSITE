New

INOX 304 stainless steel press fittings

We are adding the Besco INOX 304 line of press fittings in the M profile to our range: up to 16 bar, from −10 to 110 °C.

  • Fast installation without a flame: joints are pressed cold, with no welding, soldering or gluing.
  • Drinking water and heating: corrosion-resistant stainless steel, CE marking, PZH hygiene certificate.
  • Full range: diameters 15–108 mm, 25 product groups and 287 items.
  • Common M profile: the fittings are pressed with M jaws, which many installers already have for their press tools.
  • Larger diameters: our range also includes INOX 316L up to 168.3 mm, with DVGW and WRAS approvals.

Technical questions

Good to know.

Answers based on the manufacturer’s documentation. Our sales team will explain the rest.

Call: 513 191 502
What does the K900 Pro series include?

Couplings, elbows, tees and female/male thread adaptors for copper pipes 8–54 mm (listed on armatex.pl as KN 900). Plus valves with compression ends and separate nuts and rings.

Does installation require tools?

Only spanners. The ring grips the pipe as the nut is tightened, without soldering or a press tool.

Are there valves in the system?

Yes: taps and valves with compression ends in diameters 15–28 mm.

How are the fittings packed?

Each item has two bulk pack sizes in the manufacturer's price list, for example straight coupling K910X 15 mm: 5 and 150 pieces.
