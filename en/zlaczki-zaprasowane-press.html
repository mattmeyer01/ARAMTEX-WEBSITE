Technical questions

Good to know.

Answers based on the manufacturer’s documentation. Our sales team will explain the rest.

Call: 513 191 502
What is the difference between the V and M profiles?

It is the shape of the press end and of the press tool jaw. Besco copper comes in both profiles (V 12–54 mm, M 12–108 mm), carbon steel in the M profile. M profile fittings can also be pressed with a V jaw in the DN12–28 range.

Which fittings are suitable for gas?

GP Gas lines in V and M profiles: diameters 15–35 mm, up to 5 bar, from −20 to 70 °C, EN 1254-7 standard, DVGW and INiG certificates. Fittings from the water lines are not used in gas installations.

What is Gutpress Carbon Steel used for?

For closed heating installations and compressed air. M profile, diameters 12–108 mm, up to 16 bar, from −10 to 110 °C.

What is the difference between INOX 304 and INOX 316L?

Both lines are stainless steel press fittings in the M profile, up to 16 bar, from −10 to 110 °C. According to Besco catalogues, INOX 316L is intended for water, heating and industry, has DVGW and WRAS approvals and a PZH certificate, and comes in diameters 15–168.3 mm. The manufacturer describes INOX 304 for heating, with CE marking, in diameters 15–108 mm.

What approvals do copper press fittings have?

V profile: DVGW, KIWA, WRAS, RISE and a PZH certificate. M profile: DVGW, WRAS, RISE and a PZH certificate. Gas lines: DVGW and an INiG certificate. All to EN 1254-7.

Which ball valves are in the system?

Gutpress Ball Valves V and M: press × press and press × female thread, 15–54 mm, also with extended stem. Up to 16 bar, from −10 to 110 °C, DVGW and WRAS approvals.
