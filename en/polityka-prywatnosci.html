New

INOX 304 stainless steel press fittings

We are adding the Besco INOX 304 line of press fittings in the M profile to our range: up to 16 bar, from −10 to 110 °C.

  • Fast installation without a flame: joints are pressed cold, with no welding, soldering or gluing.
  • Drinking water and heating: corrosion-resistant stainless steel, CE marking, PZH hygiene certificate.
  • Full range: diameters 15–108 mm, 25 product groups and 287 items.
  • Common M profile: the fittings are pressed with M jaws, which many installers already have for their press tools.
  • Larger diameters: our range also includes INOX 316L up to 168.3 mm, with DVGW and WRAS approvals.

Privacy policy.

Rules for processing personal data and information on cookies on the armatex.pl website. Effective from 10 October 2026.

This policy describes what personal data we collect through the armatex.pl website, for what purpose, how long we keep it and what rights you have. It also describes what information the website stores in your browser.

1. Data controller

The controller of personal data is P.H.U. ARMATEX Sp. J. A. J. Bunda, with its registered office in Olsztyn, ul. Siewna 24, 10-831 Olsztyn, entered in the register of entrepreneurs of the National Court Register under KRS number 0000371628, NIP 7393814110, REGON 280563743 (hereinafter: “Armatex”).

For matters relating to personal data, you can contact us:

  • by e-mail: biuro@armatex.pl,
  • by phone: 513 191 502,
  • by post: ul. Składowa 3a, 10-421 Olsztyn (office and warehouse) or to the registered office address.

2. What data we collect

Enquiry form. When you submit the form, we receive the data you enter in it: your e-mail address and telephone number (required) and, optionally, your name, company name, company profile, town or province and the content of your enquiry, including the list of items for a quote.

E-mail and telephone contact. When you write to or call us directly, we process the data provided in that correspondence or conversation.

Technical data. Each time a page is displayed, the hosting server automatically records technical data such as the IP address, date and time, the address of the subpage visited and the browser type. We do not use it to identify individuals.

Visit statistics. We measure website traffic with Plausible Analytics. It collects aggregate data about visits: the address of the subpage visited, the address of the page you came from, the browser, operating system and device type, and approximate location (country, region and city determined from the IP address). It also counts selected actions on the website: clicking a phone number or e-mail address, downloading a file (e.g. a PDF catalogue), clicking a link to another website, opening the news window, adding a product to the quote list, moving from the list to the form, the choice in the cookie consent window, submitting the form (only the company profile, the number of items from the list and the button the enquiry came from, without contact details or the text entered in the form) and opening a page that does not exist (404 error). Plausible does not use cookies, does not store anything in your browser and does not keep the IP address. It recognises visits on the same day by a hash of the IP address and browser data, calculated with a key that is deleted and replaced every 24 hours. As a result, the statistics cannot be used to establish who you are or to recognise you on a visit the next day.

Google Tag Manager. The website uses Google Tag Manager, a tool we use to load additional scripts (tags) on the website. Your browser downloads them from Google servers, which receive your IP address and browser data in the process. According to Google, Tag Manager itself does not retain data about visitors other than standard server logs, which are deleted within 14 days. Google tags that can store cookies (e.g. Google Analytics or Google Ads) are set by default to “consent denied” in Google consent mode and in this mode do not store cookies. They store them only after you give consent in the cookie consent window (section 8).

Providing data is voluntary, but without an e-mail address and telephone number we will not be able to respond to an enquiry sent via the form.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell data. Access to it is limited to authorised Armatex employees and to entities that provide services to us and process data on our behalf:

  • Netlify, Inc.: website hosting (including recording technical data) and handling of the form: it stores submitted enquiries and sends notifications to our e-mail inbox,
  • Plausible Insights OÜ (Estonia): website visit statistics; the data is processed and stored in the European Union,
  • Google Ireland Limited (Ireland) and Google LLC (USA): Google Tag Manager (IP address and browser data when the page loads),
  • e-mail providers that run our inbox,
  • GitHub, Inc.: storage of the website code and a copy of the website on GitHub Pages (technical data when that copy is displayed),
  • accounting firm, courier and transport companies, IT providers: to the extent necessary to fulfil orders and run the business.

We may also disclose data to public authorities where required by law.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Some service providers (Netlify, GitHub, Google, e-mail providers) may process data outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the United States. This takes place on the basis of mechanisms provided for in the GDPR, in particular a European Commission adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission. We will provide information on the safeguards applied on request.

6. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that did not lead to cooperation: up to 12 months from the last contact, or until consent is withdrawn or an objection is successfully raised, if earlier.
  • Data related to orders and contracts: for the duration of our cooperation, and then until the limitation periods for claims expire and for the period required by tax and accounting regulations (as a rule, 5 years from the end of the tax year).
  • Form submissions in the Netlify panel: we delete them periodically, at the latest within the periods given above for enquiries.
  • Visit statistics (Plausible): aggregate visit counts, without IP addresses; the key used to calculate the IP address hash is deleted every 24 hours.
  • Google Tag Manager: standard Google server logs, deleted within 14 days according to Google.
  • Technical data in hosting logs: for the period set by the hosting provider, usually no longer than a few weeks.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • rectification of data,
  • erasure of data,
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • withdraw consent at any time; withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

To exercise these rights, write to biuro@armatex.pl. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the President of the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO) (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl).

8. Cookies and browser storage

We store cookies only with your consent. On your first visit, we ask for it in a window at the bottom of the page. You can accept all cookies, reject them or choose categories: statistics (e.g. Google Analytics) and marketing (e.g. Google Ads: measuring the effectiveness of ads and ads tailored to you). Without consent, Google tags run in “consent denied” mode and do not store cookies, and the website works the same.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time with the “Cookie settings” button in the page footer. When you withdraw consent, we delete the statistics and advertising cookies previously stored in your browser. Plausible visit statistics work without cookies and do not require consent (section 2).

The website uses browser storage (localStorage) for three purposes:

  • the quote list that you create with the “Add” button in the catalogue and on product pages: article numbers, product names, quantities and units. The list stays only on your device and is not sent to us until you transfer it to the form and submit it,
  • the closed news bar at the top of the page: we store only the identifier of the message so that it does not appear again after you close it,
  • your choice in the cookie consent window: for 12 months, so that we do not ask on every visit; after that we will ask again.

These are functions you request, so they do not require separate consent (Art. 399 of the Electronic Communications Law Act).

We delete the list automatically after the form is submitted. You can also delete it at any time: with the “×” button next to the items on the list or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

9. External content and services

Website files (texts, images, fonts, scripts) are loaded from our hosting. The exceptions are the Plausible statistics script, loaded from the plausible.io server, and Google Tag Manager with the tags configured in it, loaded from Google servers (section 2). The website contains links to other services, e.g. uodo.gov.pl. The owners of those services are responsible for their privacy policies.

10. Security

The website is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Access to enquiry data is limited to the people who need it to serve customers.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, e.g. when we add new website features. The current version is always available on this page. If we add new tools that require your consent, we will describe them here and ask for your consent before enabling them.
