Questions

Partners' questions.

From wholesalers, installation companies and contractors. Technical questions about individual systems are answered on their pages.

Call: 513 191 502
How quickly will I get a quote?

Within one working day. The quote includes wholesale prices, availability and delivery times for the systems or items you want to stock.

Do you only work with wholesalers?

Plumbing wholesalers are our main partners, and we set our stock, logistics and terms around them. We also serve installation companies, general contractors, industrial plants and building managers, especially for larger investments and project orders. Tell us what you need and we will find the right form of partnership.

Which brands and systems do you distribute?

Besco Fittings & Connectors: Gutpress Copper in V and M profiles, gas series, Gutpress Carbon Steel, INOX 304 and 316L stainless steel press, solder fittings and Gutpress Ball Valves V and M. Pegler Yorkshire: Tectite push-fit fittings, Kuterlite compression fittings and push-fit valves. Around 2 900 items in total.

What is the delivery time?

Shipping from the Olsztyn warehouse within 24 hours of order confirmation. We deliver large orders with our own transport.

Can I order by article number?

Yes. Enter the numbers in the enquiry form or build a list in the product finder: 3 462 Besco, Tectite and Kuterlite items grouped by line. Add items, enter quantities and send for a quote.

How do I get access to the documents?

Request access via the form. Once your company has been verified, we provide data sheets, certificates and declarations within one working day.
