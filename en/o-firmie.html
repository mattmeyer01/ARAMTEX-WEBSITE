About us

Armatex. Fittings distributor for wholesalers.

We have been in business since 1991 and are the official representative of Pegler Yorkshire in Poland. From our warehouse in Olsztyn we supply Besco and Pegler Yorkshire fittings and valves to wholesalers throughout Poland.

What we do

A wholesaler for plumbing wholesalers.

We do not sell to project owners and do not carry out installations. Our customers are wholesalers, so our entire range, logistics and service are geared to stocking them: full manufacturer packs, stock of the fastest-moving items and one invoice for the whole range.

01 Four jointing methods

Press, push-fit, compression and solder fittings from two manufacturers. Over 3 200 items in one place.

02 Warehouse in Olsztyn

ul. Składowa 3a. We pick a typical wholesaler order the same day, with dispatch within 24 hours of confirmation.

03 Quote in one day

We respond to enquiries within one working day: prices, availability and delivery times.
